Depth turns this category's total SF into cubic yards automatically (SF × depth ÷ 12 ÷ 27), plus waste if set.

Start a new project?

This clears all measurements, categories, scales and the loaded plan. If you haven't saved, save first — otherwise this work is gone (one undo step can bring the measurements back).

🕘 Project versions

Snapshots of this project: measurements, categories, scales and plans. Save one at milestones like "Bid v1" or "Revised scope". Autosaves are kept too — every 10 minutes while you work, and before big changes. Restore any of them anytime.
